An oreum is a small volcanic hill, and more than four hundred of them are scattered across Jeju Island. None are tall, so anyone can climb one, and no two look alike. We make games the way you climb an oreum: easy to pick up, and genuinely fun.

People You Meet on the Island
  • Lulu Protagonist

    Thirty years old. Ten years of saving in Seoul still couldn't buy a home, so she spent everything she had on an empty house in a remote Jeju village.

  • The Mayor Runs the Mayor's Store and owns the land

    He buys your tangerines, leases out fields, and sells seeds and furniture. Most of the money on this island passes through his hands.

  • Granny the Haenyeo Keeper of the harbor

    She sits along the path to the water and has a word for you every time you pass. "Get greedy, and the sea will take you."

  • Munam Lives on the island's southern shore

    He doesn't seem to do anything, yet somehow carries himself with class. Even deep in the Jeju countryside, he wears a suit every single day.
