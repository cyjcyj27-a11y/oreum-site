FAQ

Do I really earn money with the tab closed?

Coin Laundry 24 keeps earning while the tab is closed, and in Making Kimchi your kimchi ferments in the meantime. You collect it when you come back.

Is there a goal?

Coin Laundry 24 is about paying off a 3,000 coin debt, and Farm on Mars is about terraforming Mars. Bungeoppang grows a street stall into a real shop.

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser. Open the game again on the same device and browser to continue.
