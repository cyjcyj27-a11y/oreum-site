FAQ

How long is a round?

Many are short. Cookie Stack, BOA and Slip Cat are good for a few quick rounds on a break.

Do they end?

Cookie Stack ends at the cookie moon above 190 floors across 10 levels, and Control King ends after climbing 24 sections from a well to the moon.

What happens to plushies I win?

Plushies you win in Toy Crane go into your collection book.
