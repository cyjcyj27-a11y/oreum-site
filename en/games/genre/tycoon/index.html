FAQ

How does the cooking work?

Read the order, pick the ingredients and grill or fry them. In Dev Chicken Shop chicken burns if you fry it too long, and in Kingwangzzang you match rare or well done and even no pickles.

Are the recipes real?

Making Kimchi follows 9 real kimchi recipes: wash, cut and salt the cabbage, then season it.

Is there an ending?

Hotteok Maker runs for 14 days with nine kinds of hotteok, and Making Kimchi grows your shop from a Bronx back alley to Manhattan.
