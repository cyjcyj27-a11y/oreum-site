FAQ

Are these puzzle games hard?

Color Mix, Double Parking Escape and Sudoku start with easy boards and get a little harder as you go. If you get stuck, stop and pick it up again later.

Which puzzle game has the most levels?

Oreumnyang has 200 stages, PIXL has 128 boards, and Color Mix and Double Parking Escape have 100 each.

Is there an escape room game?

Yes, The Locked Room. Solve 20 stages across 5 rooms of a missing novelist's mansion, collect 12 clues and name the culprit.
