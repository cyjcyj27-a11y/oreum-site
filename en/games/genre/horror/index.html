FAQ

What do you do in these games?

In Mulgwisin you climb the valley finding 8 talismans and traces of your sister. In Night Study you get out of an abandoned school.

When does it get dangerous?

In Mulgwisin, the water ghost comes if your lantern goes out or you stay in the water too long.

Do they work on phones?

Yes. They work on PC and mobile.
