FAQ

Who do I play Ddakji against?

24 neighborhood kids in alleys, playgrounds, schoolyards and outside the stationery shop. You collect 36 kinds of ddakji.

How many people can use the Ladder Game?

From 2 to 50 people, and you can send the result as a link.

How does Yut Nori work?

Throw four sticks to get do, gae, geol, yut or mo, and the first to bring all four pieces home wins. Play against 12 opponents or with up to four people on one phone.
