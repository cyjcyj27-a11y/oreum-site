FAQ

How did you sort the genres?

We played each game and sorted it by what you actually do in it. If a game fits two genres, it is in both. Gomoku, for example, is a board game and a Korean traditional game, and two people can play it.

Which genre has the most games?

2 Player Games lead with 14 games, followed by puzzle games with 13 and board games with 10.

I do not know what to play. Any recommendations?

If you are new, start with puzzle or arcade games that have one-line rules. For something to sink time into, try idle or adventure games. With a friend next to you, pick a 2 player game.

Is everything free?

Yes. You can play to the end without paying, with no install and no sign-up.

Do they work on phones?

Yes. They work on PC and mobile. Tap on a phone, keyboard and mouse on a PC.
