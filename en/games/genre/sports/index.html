FAQ

Can two people play?

Finger Kick, Foosball, 3D Bowling, Pocket Ball and Jeju Kart Racing all work two players on one screen.

Are the rules like the real sport?

Pocket Ball uses 8 ball rules, and Finger Kick is 5 on 5 with 3 minute matches.

Is there a racing game?

Yes, Jeju Kart Racing, a 3D kart racer with four animal drivers on 12 Jeju tracks.
