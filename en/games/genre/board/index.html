FAQ

How strong is the opponent?

Chess and Janggi go from 18 kyu to 9 dan. Gomoku, Baduk and Checkers go up one level per win until 9 dan. The first opponent is beatable for beginners.

Can two people play?

Yes. Chess, Janggi, Checkers, Gomoku, Baduk, Alkkagi, Land Grab and Wooden Block work two players on one screen, and Yut Nori takes up to four on one phone.

Are the boards real size?

Gomoku uses a 15-line board and Baduk a 13-line board. Chess follows the full rules, including castling, en passant and promotion.
