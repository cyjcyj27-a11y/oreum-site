COIN LAUNDRY 24

🎮 Play now

Coin Laundry 24 is a free idle laundromat game: you run a 24-hour coin laundromat and pay off a 3,000-coin debt. It runs in your browser with nothing to install.

It is 6 p.m. and you have just taken over a coin laundromat that comes with a 3,000-coin debt. Customers walk in on their own, drop coins into a washer and start it. The coins pile up inside each machine, and when you tap a washer they burst out and fly into your wallet. Tap a finished load to fold it onto the table, and the customer picks it up and leaves a tip. A broken washer takes three taps to fix, and the drunk, the kicker and the thief who show up between 2 and 5 a.m. get thrown out with a tap.

The shop sells eighteen upgrades. Washers go up to eight, and the fast motor and premium soap raise the speed and the price. A rack adds room to stack folded laundry, so clean loads never pile up. The owner walks the shop and repairs, mops and folds even with no upgrades at all. The toolbox and the mop make him faster, and the coin cart hands him the collecting too, so you can take your hands off. CCTV keeps the thief away and the neon sign brings more customers. Junho the clerk runs the shop, says something different depending on the hour and the weather, and knocks the prices down once he likes you.

Customers drop laundry off while the tab is closed. Come back and every washer has coins waiting, with the puddles and breakdowns from the night still there. Collecting and cleaning is your first round of work. Sometimes a banknote or a lost ring turns up. Reputation stars decide how many customers come, and regulars build up to five hearts. A day is six minutes, seasons change every seven days, and it rains and snows. There is no fail state. Pay off the debt and you get the DEBT FREE ending, and after that you open branches 2, 3 and 4 to multiply the income.

FAQ

Do I need to install Coin Laundry 24?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app, no sign-up.

Is Coin Laundry 24 free?

Yes, completely free. No payments.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Turn the phone sideways and tap the washers and customers with your finger. On a PC you click with the mouse.

How do I play?

Tap a washer to collect its coins, tap finished laundry to fold it, and tap a broken washer three times to fix it. Tap the late-night troublemakers to throw them out and tap a puddle twice to mop it. The shop button at the bottom right sells upgrades.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically in your browser. Open it again and Continue on the title screen picks up where you left off. Nothing is sent to a server.

Do I keep earning while the tab is closed?

Yes. Customers leave laundry in overnight, so when you come back every washer has run one load. Tap the machines to collect the coins. Time away does not matter, only how many washers you own.

Can it run by itself?

The owner walks the shop and works on his own. Even before you buy anything, he repairs broken washers, mops up puddles and folds finished loads. The toolbox and the mop make him do the same jobs faster, and the coin cart hands him the collecting too. Without the cart he only empties washers whose coin box is full, so until then tapping to collect is quicker.

What about the troublemakers?

They come between 2 and 5 a.m. The drunk throws up and blocks a washer, the kicker breaks washers, and the thief runs off with a coin box or a pile of laundry. Tap him before he reaches the door and he drops it. Buy CCTV and the thief stops coming.

How does it end?

There is no fail state. Pay the 3,000-coin debt from the shop window and you get the DEBT FREE ending, and after that you open branches 2, 3 and 4 and keep going.
