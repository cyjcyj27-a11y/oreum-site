FARTLIKE

🎮 Play now

FARTLIKE is a free fart game where a girl eats Korean food and farts away swarms of enemies, survivors-like style. It runs in your browser with no download, on PC and mobile.

Mosquitoes, pigeons and puppies swarm in. Little toots go off on their own and push back whatever gets close. Hold the fart button and gas builds up in her belly; let go and one big fart blows away everything around you. Let go the moment the gas is full for a PERFECT and it hits even harder.

Hold it too long and BRRRT. It blows much bigger, but she gets dizzy for a moment and can't move. When to let go is the whole game.

Every level up you eat one of three food cards. Sweet potato fills the gas faster, stinky bean stew makes the smell cloud last longer, milk makes big farts stronger, garlic gives you an aura. There are 14 foods, and max out two of them for a golden combo card. Fire Noodles + Lighter makes a Fire Fart with purple lightning. Rocket Fart, Cereal Bomb and Hell Cloud are in there too, six combos in all.

Five stages: School Field, Subway, Sauna, Theme Park and Air Freshener Factory. A nagging auntie, gas-mask janitors, rolling sauna eggs, water gun kids and bear mascots show up in turn, and survive 8 minutes to meet the boss. Beat King Pigeon, Gas-Mask Chief, King Sauna Egg, Bear Boss and Skunk King to open the next stage; the fifth boss is the ending.

Coins from each run buy permanent upgrades in the shop: HP, power, shoes, magnet, revive and more. Lose, get stronger, try again. A run takes about 10 minutes, and CONTINUE picks up where you left off.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install FARTLIKE?

No. Open the link and it starts in your browser. No app to install and no sign-up.

Is FARTLIKE free?

Yes, completely free. No payments.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It works on mobile in portrait or landscape, and on PC. On a phone, move with the left pad, then hold the fart button on the right and let go for a big fart.

How do I play?

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Space to build up gas and let go for a big fart. Little toots go off on their own.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to continue. Nothing is sent to a server.

What is BRRRT?

It's what happens if you hold a full belly of gas too long. It blows bigger, but you get dizzy for a moment and can't move.

How long is a run?

Survive 8 minutes and the boss shows up; with the boss, a run is about 10 minutes. Clear all five stages for the ending.

Who made it?

Oreum Games, a small indie game studio in Korea.
