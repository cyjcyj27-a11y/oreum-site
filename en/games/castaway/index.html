OPEN SEA

🎮 Play now

Open Sea is a free raft fishing survival game: stranded at sea, you catch fish to eat and to use as bait. It runs in your browser with nothing to install.

Water in every direction. All you have is a raft, a fishing rod and a hunger bar that keeps dropping. Hold space to build up power and let go to cast, set the hook the moment the float dips, and tap to reel it in. Every fish you land can be eaten to fill the bar or cut up and used as bait. When hunger hits zero, it is over.

Fishing has its own logic here. A bare hook only takes the small fish under the raft, anchovy and damselfish. Bait the hook with one of those and better fish come looking: red snapper, striped beakfish, yellowtail. Bait it with a good fish and cast far, and big fish like bluefin tuna and blue marlin appear. The real work starts once a big one is on. Watch the tension bar, wait while the fish runs, and reel when it stops. Keep reeling through a run and the line snaps.

Days pass and night falls, and cutlassfish and conger eel only bite in the dark. There are 30 species in all. Each catch goes into your fish log with its name and your biggest size, while the ones you have not caught yet show as silhouettes. Fill in the other 29 and the great white shark appears last; land it and the man makes it back to shore, with the days you survived and your biggest fish on record.

FAQ

Do I need to install Open Sea?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app, no sign-up.

Is Open Sea free?

Yes, completely free. No payments.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Turn the phone sideways. One button at the bottom right casts, hooks and reels, and you drag the screen to look around. On a PC it is the space bar and the mouse.

How do I play?

Hold space to build up power and release to cast. When the float dips, press space to set the hook, then tap repeatedly to reel. When the fish runs, stop reeling and wait, or the line snaps.

Is my record saved?

Your best run in days, your biggest fish and your fish log are saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser and they are still there. Nothing is sent to a server.

How does it end?

When your hunger bar hits zero it is GAME OVER. Log all 29 other species and the great white shark appears; land it and you make it back to shore: CLEAR. The fish log is kept between runs, and CONTINUE on the title screen picks up the run you left.

How do I catch the big fish?

A bare hook only takes small fish. Bait it with a small fish you caught and better fish such as red snapper and yellowtail come in; bait it with one of those and cast far, and the big ones show up: sailfish, bluefin tuna, blue marlin and swordfish. One bait fish is cut into 2 to 40 pieces depending on its size, so it lasts several casts: the smallest, an anchovy, gives 2 and the biggest, a swordfish, 40. Only the great white shark cannot be used as bait.

How many kinds of fish are there?

30. Anchovy bite first under the raft, then damselfish, horse mackerel, sardine and mackerel open up one after another, then gizzard shad, rockfish, filefish, halfbeak, pufferfish and cutlassfish. With bait on the hook come black seabream, striped beakfish, opaleye, red snapper, flounder, sea bass, conger eel, Spanish mackerel, croaker, cod, grouper, mahi-mahi, yellowtail and amberjack; cast far for sailfish, bluefin tuna, blue marlin and swordfish. The thirtieth and last is the great white shark. Cutlassfish and conger eel only come at night. Each fish you catch goes in the log with its name and your biggest size; the ones you have not caught show as silhouettes.
