FOOSBALL

🎮 Play now

FOOSBALL is a free online foosball game: a 3D table soccer table where you slide the rods and flick to kick. It runs in your browser with no download, on PC and mobile.

Your four rods slide together as far as your hand pushes, and one press kicks with all of them. Each rod is a steel bar in bearings: a tap flicks the men up and they swing back down, and holding the kick keeps the wrist turning so a foot comes over the top and sweeps a ball forward from behind the men. The ball goes wherever the foot is moving, so own goals happen just like on a real table.

On your own you play 60 stages, five against each of 12 national teams: Japan, Mexico, USA, Netherlands, Portugal, Croatia, Italy, Spain, Germany, France, Argentina and Brazil. Opponents react faster, aim better and kick harder as you go. Beat stage 60 for the CHAMPION ending, and the tour keeps going after that. Pick 2 PLAYERS on PC to play a friend on one keyboard.

Every goal earns coins. Spend them on 11 more kits, from red and green up to Panther, White Tiger, Gold and Galaxy, each with more kick power and rod speed. There are 12 missions such as a keeper goal, a bank shot goal, a comeback win and 5 wins in a row.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install FOOSBALL?

No. Open the link and it starts in your browser. There is nothing to download and no sign-up.

Is FOOSBALL free?

Yes, it is completely free. You can play to the end without paying.

How do I play foosball online here?

On PC move the mouse up and down or press the up and down arrows to slide the rods, and click or press SPACE to kick. On a phone drag the left side of the screen up and down and tap KICK at the bottom right. Hold the kick to keep the men spinning and pull a ball forward from behind them.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It works on phones held sideways and on PC. The 2 player mode is PC only.

Is the physics like a real foosball table?

Yes. The rods slide only as far as you push and stop where a man touches the wall. The men swing like pendulums and the ball goes wherever the foot is moving. There is no auto-aim that lines a man up with the ball.

Who do I play against?

12 national teams from Japan to Brazil, five stages each, 60 in all. First to 3 goals wins up to stage 12, then 4, then 5.

Can two people play?

Yes, on PC. Pick 2 PLAYERS to play on one keyboard: player 1 uses W, S and D, player 2 uses the up and down arrows and the left arrow.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent to a server.
