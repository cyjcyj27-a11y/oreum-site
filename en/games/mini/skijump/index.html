SKI JUMP

🎮 Play now

Ski Jump is a free ski game: steer with the left and right keys, hit the jump ramp and fly. Touch a tree, rock or log once and it is over on the spot. 100 stages, and it runs in your browser with nothing to install.

There are only two controls, left and right, but they do two jobs. On the snow they move you sideways: the harder you lean, the faster you slide across and the slower you go downhill. In the air the same two keys spin you. Every full spin scores points, spins on landing after landing build a combo up to ×5, and a clean landing with no spin still pays a little.

Each stage has a goal distance, from 150 m on stage 1 to 790 m on stage 100, and the slope gets busier as you go. Coins lie along the course, but you only keep them if you cross the finish line. One touch of a tree, rock or log and the run ends where you stand, so every ramp is a choice between playing it safe and going for the spin.

FAQ

Do I need to install Ski Jump?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app, no sign-up.

Is Ski Jump free?

Yes, completely free. No payments and no ads to watch.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It is made for a portrait phone screen, with two arrow buttons at the bottom you can reach with one thumb. On a PC use the left and right arrow keys or A and D.

How do I play?

Only left and right. On the snow they move you sideways: the harder you lean, the faster you slide across and the slower you go downhill. In the air the same keys spin you; each full spin scores points and spins in a row build a combo. Reach the goal distance to clear the stage. Touch a tree, rock or log once and the run is over.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The stages you have unlocked, your best score on each stage and your coins are saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to continue. Nothing is sent to a server.
