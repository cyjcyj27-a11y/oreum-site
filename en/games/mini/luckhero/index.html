LUCKY HERO

🎮 Play now

LUCKY HERO is a dice deckbuilder where every card you play rolls a die. It is a free browser game that starts right away on phone or PC, with no download.

Your hero plays a card and a die rolls. Slash deals 3 + the die, Guard blocks 3 + the die, so the number on the card adds the pip you roll. Roll a 6 and it is a CRIT that doubles the effect. Roll a 1 and it is a MISS: the hero trips over and nothing happens. Enemies roll dice over their heads too, and when they roll a 1 they whiff as well.

A miss is never wasted. Every miss adds a leaf to the four-leaf clover in the bottom left, and once all four leaves are in, the next die is a guaranteed 7, a JACKPOT that triples the effect. The more you miss, the closer the big hit. Some cards play with luck itself, like the Lucky Coin that adds 2 to your next roll and Bounce Back that gives you Strength every time you miss.

Climb three acts, Green Woods, Moonlit Graveyard and the Demon Castle, seven floors each. On every floor you pick one of two or three doors: a fight, an elite, a treasure chest, the gacha machine, a campfire or a fate event, and the Goblin King, the Lich and the Demon Lord wait at the end of each act. Win to add a card to your deck, and pull the gacha machine for gold cards and 11 relics. When you fall, your coins go to the bank, where the luck shop sells 6 permanent upgrades like more max HP and first-turn block. The collection has 73 entries for cards, relics and monsters.

Frequently asked questions

What happens to a card when I roll a 1?

A dice card that rolls a 1 does nothing and the hero trips over. In return the clover gets one leaf. With the Ladybug relic you heal 2 HP on a miss, and with the Skull Ring every miss deals 5 damage to all enemies.

When does the JACKPOT hit?

When all four leaves are in, your next die rolls a 7 and the effect is tripled. After a jackpot the leaves start again from zero. With the Clover Pot relic three leaves are enough.

What do cards without a die icon do?

Cards with no die icon in the top right corner do not roll, they just take effect. The Lucky Coin adds 2 to your next roll and then goes to the discard pile.

Do I start over when I die?

That run starts again from Act 1, Floor 1. Your coins plus 3 coins for every floor you climbed go to the bank, and the luck shop sells permanent upgrades like max HP, first-turn block and starting coins so the next run starts stronger.

How much does the gacha machine cost?

The first pull in a run costs 25 coins and every pull after that costs 15 more. You get a normal card, a gold card or a relic.

How do I see the true ending?

Defeat the Demon Lord to see the ending and a result card. Defeat him with all 73 collection entries filled and you get the true ending, God of Luck. You can save or share the result card.
