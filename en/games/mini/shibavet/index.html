SHIBA VET DAY

🎮 Play now

SHIBA VET DAY is a free flash-style game where you catch a Shiba Inu that refuses to go to the vet and get it into the car. No install, it starts right in your browser on phone and PC.

You start in the yard. Get close to the shiba and pounce to pick it up, then carry it to the car to finish the stage. It wriggles in your arms, and when the bar fills it slips free, so pet it on the way. When it runs into the house, close doors to trap it or sneak up on it.

Every stage one treat or toy is hidden somewhere in the house. Pick it up and use it in front of the shiba: it is easy to catch while it eats a treat or brings a toy back to you. Unused items carry over to the next stage.

Each stage the shiba gets faster and notices you from farther away; later it eats and runs, or grabs the toy and bolts. There is no last stage. The stage you reach is your record.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install SHIBA VET DAY?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app to install, no account to create.

Is SHIBA VET DAY free?

Yes, completely free. There are no payments.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in landscape. The round pad at the bottom left walks (push it all the way to run), dragging the screen looks around, and the buttons at the bottom right grab. On PC it is WASD and the mouse.

What are the controls?

Get close to the shiba and press grab to pounce. Once you hold it, pet it before the wriggle bar fills and carry it to the car. You can close doors to block its way, and sneaking makes it notice you later.

What are the treats and toys?

Every stage one treat or toy is hidden somewhere in the house. Pick it up and use it in front of the shiba: it is easy to catch while it eats a treat or brings a toy back to you. Unused items carry over, and on higher stages the shiba eats and runs or steals the toy.

How many stages are there?

There is no last stage. Each stage the shiba gets faster, notices you from farther away and escapes your arms sooner. The stage you reach is your record.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser and you continue from that stage with the items you collected. Nothing is sent to a server.
