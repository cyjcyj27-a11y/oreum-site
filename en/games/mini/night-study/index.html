NIGHT STUDY

🎮 Play now

NIGHT STUDY is a free first-person horror escape game. You dozed off at your desk during evening study hall. When you open your eyes, the school is empty. Six exam papers are scattered through the building, and you have to gather them and get out through the front door on the first floor — but something in a white gown is walking the halls.

The school is a real 3D building: four classrooms along the north corridor, a science room, a music room and a library in the middle, and the staff room, front hall and art room to the south. Papers and spare batteries lie in the rooms. The front door stays locked until you have all six papers.

Your flashlight lets you see down the corridors, but it burns battery and the ghost notices its light from twice as far away. Walk in the dark when you can. If she spots you, run — running drains your breath, so duck into a locker before it runs out. If she saw you go in, she will come and check.

Three difficulties change how fast she moves, how far she sees and hears, and how long she keeps hunting once she loses you. Escape without ever turning the flashlight on and the ending screen says so.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install NIGHT STUDY?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app to install, no account to create.

Is NIGHT STUDY free?

Yes, completely free. There are no payments and no ads to watch.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Turn your phone to landscape. A stick in the bottom-left corner moves you (push it all the way to run), swiping the screen looks around, and the LIGHT and TAKE buttons sit on the right. Shadows are off by default on phones to keep it smooth.

What are the controls?

On PC: W A S D to walk, mouse to look, Shift to run, F for the flashlight, E to pick up papers or hide in a locker, Esc to pause. Running uses your breath, and the flashlight drains its battery and makes the ghost spot you from twice as far. Batteries lying around recharge it.

Is my progress saved?

No. Each run is short and starts fresh from the classroom, so there is nothing to save. Nothing is sent to a server.
