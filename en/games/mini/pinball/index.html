PINBALL 3D Pinball Game

🎮 Play now

PINBALL is a 3D pinball game where you flip the ball with the left and right flippers to clear a different goal on each of 10 stages. It runs in your browser on phone and PC with nothing to install.

We rebuilt the pinball machine that stood in the corner of the arcade: a tilted playfield, a lit backbox, chrome rails with LED strips, and a steel ball rolling over all of it in 3D. Tap the left or right side of the screen to flip, and hold the launch button, then let go, to send the ball up the lane. On PC use the arrow keys and Space.

There are 10 stages, each with its own goal: hit the bumpers 15 times, light all three P I N lanes, knock down 4 drop targets, shoot the clear ramps twice, sink the hole twice, hit 3 red targets, loop the left orbit and the ramps, sink the hole 3 times to start multiball and score 2 jackpots, lanes plus drop targets, and finally the gold target on stage 10, which swings left and right and gets faster with every hit. Hit it 12 times to win.

You start with 3 balls and earn an extra ball after stages 3, 6 and 9. For 10 seconds after each launch, a drained ball is saved and relaunched. Lighting all three lanes raises your multiplier up to 5x, and shooting the left and right ramps back to back is a combo. Beat the gold target for the Pinball Champion ending and a result card you can save or share.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when the ball drains down the middle?

If the ball falls between the flippers or down an outlane, you lose one ball. You start with 3 balls and get an EXTRA BALL after clearing stages 3, 6 and 9. When all balls are gone it is game over, and you can continue from that stage.

When is ball save active?

For 10 seconds after each launch. If the ball drains in that time, BALL SAVED appears and the ball is launched again. Ball save also turns on for 12 seconds when multiball starts and 6 seconds after you clear a stage.

How do I start multiball?

On stage 8, sink the ball into the hole three times. MULTIBALL starts and three balls fly around the table at once. Sinking the hole during multiball is a 250,000 point jackpot, and two jackpots clear the stage.

What is the skill shot?

When you launch, one of the three top lanes P I N blinks. Control the launch strength so the ball drops into the blinking lane for a 100,000 point skill shot. A shorter press launches the ball more softly.

How do the ramps work?

Shoot hard into a ramp entrance and the ball rides up the clear ramp, loops around and comes back down. A weak shot rolls back out of the entrance. Shooting the left and right ramps within 7 seconds of each other is a 75,000 point COMBO.

How do I beat the gold target?

On stage 10 a gold target rises in the middle of the table and swings left and right. Hit it 12 times with the flippers, and it speeds up with every hit. Beating it gives 1,000,000 points plus 200,000 for each ball you have left, then the Pinball Champion ending and a result card.
