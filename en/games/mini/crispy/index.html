CRISPY

🎮 Play now

CRISPY is a free hex cracker puzzle: tap groups of same-flavor crackers to break them all at once. It runs in your browser with nothing to install.

Tap any spot where two or more crackers of the same flavor touch and the whole group goes crispy! at once. The gaps fill with new crackers dropping in from the top. The bigger the group, the bigger the score: 2 crackers are worth 20 points, 5 are worth 200, and 10 are worth 900.

Break 7 or more at once and a golden cracker appears in their place; tap it to break everything within two cells. Each stage gives you 15 taps to beat the goal score. Stages 1 to 3 use three flavors, and grape joins from stage 4. Clear all 30 stages to finish. Run out of taps and you retry that stage, not from the start.

FAQ

Do I need to install CRISPY?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app, no sign-up.

Is CRISPY free?

Yes, completely free. No payments.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The board is laid out tall to fit a phone screen, and it plays on a PC too. Use your finger on a phone and the mouse on a PC.

How do I play?

Tap any spot where two or more crackers of the same flavor touch, and the whole group breaks at once. New crackers drop in from the top. Bigger groups score far more, and breaking 7 or more at once leaves a golden cracker; tap it to break everything within two cells. Beat the goal score within 15 taps to clear the stage.

Is my progress saved?

Your best stage and your sound settings are saved in your browser; the best stage shows on the GAME OVER screen. A new game always starts from stage 1. Nothing is sent to a server.
