PAIRBOOM!!

🎮 Play now

Tap two of the same picture to clear them. The path between them must run through empty cells only and may turn at most twice — it is allowed to go around the outside of the board. 100 stages in one line. Each clear brings more kinds of picture, so any one picture gets scarcer. There are 48 pictures in all, and at stage 100 each of the 48 appears exactly once as a pair. Some stages narrow the board — those are the ones that gained a picture, so they are harder, not easier. From 2:50 per stage.
