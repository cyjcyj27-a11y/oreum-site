MOE MOE MAID CAFE

🎮 Play now

MOE MOE MAID CAFE is a free idle tycoon game where you scout 12 maids and raise a maid cafe from #12 in Akiba to #1. It runs in your browser with no download, on PC and mobile.

It is an idle game, but your hands stay busy. Tap a maid while she cooks and the dish is done on the spot; hit the PERFECT zone on the gauge for extra hearts. Tap a maid who is resting for moe moe kyun: hearts go up and her skill grows by 1% each time. One hundred taps double her skill.

There are 12 maids, each with one skill. Yui walk +15%, Rin cook +25%, Mio walk +35%, Hana patience +30%, Saki cook +45%, Nene hearts +40%, Aoi price +25%, Momo hearts +60%, Reina price +50%, Kuro cook +70%, Shiro hearts +90%, Akari PERFECT zone x2. Only three work at a time, so choosing the shift is the strategy, and skills grow with level-ups and moe moe kyun.

Coins left by customers pile up on the tables, and you tap them to collect. Buy the Lucky Cat in the shop and they are collected automatically every 5 seconds. The menu starts with omurice and coffee and grows to parfait, cake and a special. Tables, kitchen, decor and sign upgrades bring more customers and higher prices, and as hearts pile up your Akiba rank climbs from #12 one step at a time.

The cafe keeps running when you close the tab. Come back after 20 minutes or more and coins are waiting on every table, sometimes with a letter or a gift. Scout all 12 maids and reach #1 in Akiba for the ending.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install MOE MOE MAID CAFE?

No. Open the link and it starts in your browser. There is nothing to download and no sign-up.

Is MOE MOE MAID CAFE free?

Yes, it is completely free. You can play to the end without paying.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It works on phones and PC. On a phone, turn it sideways and tap the maids; on PC, click them.

How do I play this idle cafe game?

Tap a cooking maid to finish the dish right away, and tap a resting maid for moe moe kyun to raise her skill. Tap the coins on the tables to collect them.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent to a server.

Do coins pile up while the tab is closed?

Yes. Come back after 20 minutes or more and coins are waiting on every table. Tap them to make them yours.

How do I get more maids?

Scout them with coins. A maid you already have never comes out again, so 12 pulls collect everyone. The price rises with every pull.

Are coins collected automatically?

At first you tap to collect. Buy the Lucky Cat in the shop and coins are collected 5 seconds after they appear. Offline coins waiting when you return are collected by tapping.
