FINGER KICK

🎮 Play now

Finger Kick is a free flick soccer game: pull a table-top player back, let go, and knock the ball into the net. It runs in your browser with nothing to install.

The five red discs are your team, the five blue ones are the CPU. On your turn, grab a player, pull it back and release; it shoots off the other way like a slingshot. If your player hits the ball, you keep the turn, up to 3 flicks in a row. Miss the ball and the CPU takes over.

The ball crossing the goal line is a goal, and the side that conceded kicks off from the centre. Walls run all round the pitch so the ball never goes out, and you are allowed to knock the other side's players around. The goalkeeper does not get pushed by the ball, so shots from a wide angle go in far more often than straight ones.

A match lasts 3 minutes. At full time you get WIN, LOSE or DRAW, and your record builds up match after match. Real stadium crowd noise and a commentator's shout play when a goal goes in.

FAQ

Do I need to install Finger Kick?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app, no sign-up.

Is Finger Kick free?

Yes, completely free. No payments and no ads to watch.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The pitch turns to fit a portrait phone or a landscape PC screen. Use your finger on a phone and the mouse on a PC to pull a player back and let go.

How do I play?

Grab one of your players, pull it back and release; it flicks the other way, harder the further you pull. If your player hits the ball you keep the turn, up to 3 flicks in a row.

Is my record saved?

Your win, draw and loss record is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to continue. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I play alone?

Yes. You play against the CPU, which simulates hundreds of possible flicks with its five players and picks the best one.
