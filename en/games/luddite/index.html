FAQ

How does the story of Luddite Church go?

A carpenter fired from a construction site because of AI meets Big Bro at a tent bar, gathers 12 followers and holds the first meeting in a basement room. He trades followers back and forth with the rival Machine Cult, takes over its Korea branch, and the story goes on through Manchester, Paris, Berlin and Detroit to the Machine Cult HQ in Silicon Valley.

How does Luddite Church end?

Open a branch at the Machine Cult HQ in Silicon Valley, fill all 80 followers in the Dex and save up 1 trillion won to be crowned Cult Leader. At the end you can save a result card with your days, followers and money.

How do you earn and spend money?

At first you earn day wages at the job site; once you have a meeting place, followers bring offerings every night. Spend it to grow from a basement room to a big tent, a 2nd-floor unit, a retreat center, an overseas mission HQ and a Grand Temple, and buy items like a merch stall or a US Congress lobby fund to speed up offerings and followers.

What happens when the Heat bar fills up?

The police raid you and you lose 20% of your followers and 30% of your money. A Cash Safe halves the money loss and a Legal Team stops all losses. Volunteering lowers Heat by 15.

Is it about a real religion?

No. The church is fictional and only borrows its name from the Luddites, the 19th century English workers who smashed machines. No real religion or group appears.
