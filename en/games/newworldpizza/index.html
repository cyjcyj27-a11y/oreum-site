NEW WORLD PIZZA

🎮 Play now

New World Pizza is a new idle game where you bake pizzas and serve all 12 world-famous guests. It runs in your browser with nothing to install.

A black limo pulls up in front of a tiny pizza shop. The guests stepping out are some of the most powerful people in the world: Donald Trump, Elon Musk, Kim Jong-un and Vladimir Putin, political leaders and billionaires who run tech giants. All of them are exaggerated satirical caricatures of real people. What they secretly buy is nothing but pizza. Above the door hangs a sign with a slice of pizza and an all-seeing eye.

Guests line up and order their favorite pizza at the counter. If an oven is free, the order goes straight in and the guest waits in front of that oven while it bakes. Tap an oven and the flames roar up so it bakes faster. The finished pizza goes into a box in the guest's hands; they drop the money in the cash box and ride off in their limo.

Money piles up in the cash box, and it only becomes yours when you tap to collect it. When the cash box is full, guests can't pay: they wait with a no-coin sign in their speech bubble, then leave. Emptying the cash box on time is the first job in this shop.

Tap a guest and they hop, and their tip chance goes up by 1%. Tip chance builds up and is saved separately for each guest. When a tip comes, 4 to 6 times the pizza price goes straight to your wallet. The more often a guest visits, the more chances you get to tap them, so looking after your regulars grows the shop faster. Every 10% you get a SKILL UP, and at 100% that guest tips on every visit.

Masked Anonymous figures gather one by one on the sidewalk out front, secretly filming the shop with phones and camcorders. Once three or more set up camp, the limos drive right past without stopping. Tap them and they run off; buy blinds in the shop and fewer show up.

You grow the shop from the shop menu. More ovens bake several pizzas at once, and firewood makes them bake faster. Every new menu puts a new pizza on the chalkboard, and the guests who love that pizza start coming.

Guests arrive in order from least to most influential, judging by their influence now and over the next ten years. That ranking follows an opinion an AI (Claude) gave while the game was being made.

The shop keeps running when you close the tab. Come back after 20 minutes or more and one round of sales per oven is waiting in the cash box, along with the Anonymous crowd that gathered while you were gone. Sometimes a guest leaves a gift envelope on the counter; tap it for a big payout.

Once you have met all 12, no more limos come, and a final G20 group order arrives. All 12 guests pack the shop at once and a giant pizza lands on the counter. Bake it and send it off, and the shop goes dark like night as fireworks burst for the ending.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests are there?

Twelve famous guests arrive one by one. A guest shows up by limo once your shop is famous enough and their favorite pizza is on the menu.

What happens when the cash box is full?

Guests get their pizza but can't pay, wait a bit, and leave. Tap the cash box to empty it, or buy a bigger one or the Auto Cash upgrade.

Who are the Anonymous people outside?

They come to secretly film the shop. If three or more gather, the limos drive past without stopping, so tap them to chase them off. Blinds from the shop keep them away.

How do tips work?

Every tap on a guest inside the shop raises that guest's tip chance by 1%. A tip pays several times the pizza price at once, and it builds up per guest, so regulars pay off most.

What happens if I close the tab and come back?

If you were away for 20 minutes or more, one round of sales per oven is waiting in the cash box, along with a crowd of Anonymous out front. Sometimes a guest leaves a gift envelope on the counter.

How does the game end?

Once you have met all 12 guests, a G20 group order comes in. Bake the giant pizza, and the game ends with fireworks.
