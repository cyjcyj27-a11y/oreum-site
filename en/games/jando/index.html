CLIFF WALKER Cliff Path Game

🎮 Play now

Cliff Walker is a 3D indie construction game where you build a plank path up a pillar of rock above the clouds, one plank at a time. It runs free in your PC or phone browser with no download.

There is no path on the stone pillar rising out of the sea of clouds. A lone worker in a straw hat spirals up the rock, laying a plank walkway as he goes. On day one, pick up the drill and hammer from the red toolbox on the ledge, hang them on your belt and get to work.

Every plank takes three steps. Drill two holes where the chalk X marks the rock, hammer in the iron stakes, then carry a plank over from the pile and set it on the stakes. Lay it square in the middle with straight stakes and you get a PERFECT and extra coins.

Holes past the end of the path are out of reach on foot. Walk off the edge, hang from your safety rope, push off the wall and swing over to the hole. Clip your carabiner to the red ring first and you dangle right there when you drop. Forget it and you slide down the cable to the last ring you clipped.

You cannot hang forever. Stay on the rope too long and it fades, thins and creaks until it snaps, and you fall all the way to the canyon floor below the clouds. From day two the dangers stack up one a day: falling rocks, gusts of wind, rain, cracked rock and thunderstorms, each one giving a warning sign like rock dust or a darkening sky before it hits.

At the end of each day, spend the coins from your planks on better tools. The drill gets faster, the hammer drives a stake in one hit and the safety rope holds longer, and each tool turns from copper to gold the moment you buy it. Along the path, 12 things left behind by the workers who came before you hang on rusty nails, and pressing E adds them to your collection.

Lay all 160 planks over ten days and you walk up to the red pavilion on the summit and turn around. The camera pulls back to show the whole road winding up above the clouds, and you get a result card with your planks, height, falls, relics and title to save or share.

On PC, walk with WASD, look around with the mouse and run with Shift. Hold E or left click to drill, hammer and push out a plank, press Space to jump or climb up onto a plank while hanging, and B opens your relics. On a phone, play in landscape with the left pad and the WORK and JUMP buttons on the right.

FAQ

How do I drill a hole far past the end of the path?

Just walk off the end and you hang from your safety rope. Push off the wall with A and D to swing, wind the rope up or down with W and S to match the hole, then hold E to drill. When you are done, press Space at the end of a plank to climb up.

Why should I clip onto the red rings?

The last ring you clipped is where you hang when you fall. Fall without clipping and you slide down the cable to that ring and have to walk back up from there. A gold circle marks the spot when there is a ring to clip.

What happens if I hang too long?

The starting safety rope holds for 45 seconds. After 28 seconds it fades, thins and starts shaking, and at 45 seconds it snaps and you fall to the canyon floor. Retry starts that day over, and upgrading the rope in the shop raises the limit to 70 and then 100 seconds.

What if I run out of planks?

You get two more planks than the day needs. A plank you are carrying is lost if you fall, and if the pile runs dry you pay 30 coins for two more.

What changes from day to day?

The planks to lay each day go up from 6 to 22, and a new danger joins in: falling rocks on day 2, wind on day 3, rain on day 4, cracked rock on day 6 and thunderstorms on day 8. Nothing on screen spells it out. Sounds, rock dust and the sky warn you first.

Where are the things the old workers left behind?

There are 12 of them hanging on rusty nails along the path, spaced out from the 4th and 15th plank onward. Walk up and press E to take one for 30 coins and a spot in your collection, which stays even when you start a new game.

How do I see the ending?

Lay all 160 planks by day ten and the ending has you walk up to the pavilion on the summit. The title on your result card depends on how many times you fell, and never falling once earns Never Fell Once.
