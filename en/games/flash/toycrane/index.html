TOY CRANE Claw Machine Game

🎮 Play now

TOY CRANE is a free browser claw machine game where you move the claw, grab a plush toy and drop it in the chute. No download, it starts right in your browser.

Move the claw with the joystick or arrow keys and press DROP to send it down for a toy. After 30 seconds of TIME it drops by itself wherever it is. The claw has three arms on hinges, so depending on where it grabs, it can hold a toy tight or let it slip.

Just like a real machine, the grip gets weaker at the top, and the claw jolts once when it hits the top and once more when it stops over the chute. A toy that falls then may roll over the others or catch on the edge of the chute. The arms are loose, so all three never close the same way.

There are 20 toys in the machine, and the count goes down each time you win one. Win the last one and 20 new toys pour in from the top. Bear, rabbit, cat, puppy, duck, penguin, pig, frog, panda, fox and dino, 11 kinds in all, fill your book, and you can drag the screen to look at the machine from the side.

Frequently asked questions

How many toys are in the machine?

There are 20 at a time. The count of toys left is shown at the top, and when you win the last one, 20 new toys fill the machine again.

Why did the claw drop my toy?

The grip gets weaker at the top, and the claw jolts when it hits the top and when it stops over the chute. Whether the toy holds on depends on where the claw grabbed it.

Is there a time limit for each try?

Press DROP within 30 seconds of TIME. When the time runs out, the claw drops by itself wherever it is.

Can I look at the machine from the side?

Yes. Drag the screen or press LEFT, FRONT or RIGHT to walk around the machine. Use the mouse wheel or spread two fingers to zoom in and follow the claw.

What goes in the book?

Every kind of toy you win. There are 11 kinds, and the book shows how many of each you have won. A kind you win for the first time shows NEW!.

Does it end?

There are no levels or ending. The machine refills when it is empty, and your count of toys won keeps going up.
