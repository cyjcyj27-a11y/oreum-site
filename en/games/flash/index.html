Why they work without Flash Player

Flash Player support ended at the end of 2020. The way games used to run right in the browser was carried on by HTML5. Every game here is made in HTML5, so it runs in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Samsung Internet without installing anything.

How they were picked

Only simple games that start as soon as you open them. The rules fit in one line. They are easy at first and get harder as you go. Rounds are short, so you can stop any time. Your progress stays only in your browser.

FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. They are all made in HTML5, so they run right away in Chrome, Edge, Safari and Samsung Internet.

Do I need to sign up or download anything?

No. Open the link and it starts. There is no app to install and no sign-up.

Are they all free?

Yes. You can play to the end without paying.

Do they work on phones?

Yes. They work on both PC and mobile. Touch on phones, keyboard and mouse on PC.

Are these only simple games?

That is how they were picked. These are 28 simple games with one-line rules and short rounds. Longer games are in the full list of free games.

Aren't they hard?

You do not need to know the rules at first. They get a little harder as you go, and you can stop and start over any time.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser and you pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent to a server.
