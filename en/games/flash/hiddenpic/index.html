Hidden Objects

🎮 Play now

Hidden Objects is a free web hidden object game: find the items on your list in cluttered 3D rooms. It runs in your browser with no download.

Six rooms built like miniatures: an attic, a stationery shop, a Korean grandma's living room, a garage, a library and a greenhouse. Shelves, tables and floors are crammed with stuff, and somewhere in there are a key, a rubber duck, an alarm clock, a crown and more. Each item on your list shows as a photo, and when you find it in the picture its photo gets a red circle.

Every room has five stages, 30 in all. You start with 6 items to find and end with 10, and later items get smaller and sometimes sit half behind other things. The fourth and fifth stage in each room take place at night under lamp light. Each stage has a time limit, a wrong tap costs 5 seconds, and finding items quickly in a row gives 3 seconds back.

Clear a stage and pick one of three cards to add an item to your 49-slot collection. The card also helps on your next stage with extra time, an extra hint or a shield against wrong taps. Gold cards turn up now and then, and if you also find the hidden cat that is not on the list, one card is always gold. Clear all 30 stages and your collection ends up on a display shelf in the final scene.

Frequently asked questions

What is the hidden cat?

Every stage hides one curled-up orange cat that is not on your list. You can clear the stage without it, but if you find it, one of the three cards at the end of that stage is always a gold card.

What happens when time runs out?

You get GAME OVER, and the items you did not find are marked with red circles in the picture. Press RETRY to play the same stage again with everything in the same place.

The items are tiny. Can I zoom in?

Yes, up to 4 times. Use the mouse wheel on PC or spread two fingers on a phone, then drag to move around. You also get two hints per stage.

What are the cards at the end of a stage?

The 49 items you can find come as cards. The card you pick goes into your collection and helps on your next stage only: 20 extra seconds, one extra hint or a shield for 2 wrong taps. Gold cards give 45 seconds, two hints or 5 shields.

If I replay a stage, are the items in the same place?

No. When you pick a stage you already cleared, the items and the clutter move to new spots, so the same room plays differently each time while you fill your collection.

What happens after stage 30?

Clearing the last stage ends the game with your collected items on a display shelf. Fill all 49 slots first and you see COMPLETE, and the cat in front of the shelf wears a crown.
