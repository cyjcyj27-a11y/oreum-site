WOODEN BLOCK

🎮 Play now

WOODEN BLOCK is a free block tower game, similar to Jenga, where you pull wooden blocks out of the tower and stack them on top. It runs in your browser with no download, on PC and mobile.

Press and drag a block to push or pull it along its length. Every block is a little different in thickness, so loose blocks slide right out, while tight ones barely move or drag the whole tower with them if you force it. Once a block is out, tap one of the glowing spots on top to stack it. You cannot take blocks from the top level, or from the level below it while the top is unfinished.

On your own, you take turns with a cat. If the cat knocks the tower down you clear the stage, and if you do it is GAME OVER. The cat gets better at finding loose blocks as the stages go up, and clearing STAGE 30 shows the ending. Pick 2 PLAYERS to take turns with a friend on one device.

Every block you stack earns coins. Spend them on 11 more block sets such as birch, walnut, marble, ice, glow and gold, for 12 in all. There are 12 missions such as pulling 50 blocks and building a 26-level tower.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install WOODEN BLOCK?

No. Open the link and it starts in your browser. There is nothing to download and no sign-up.

Is WOODEN BLOCK free?

Yes, it is completely free. You can play to the end without paying.

Is it like Jenga?

The rules are similar: pull blocks out of the tower one at a time, stack them on top, and whoever topples the tower loses. The blocks also differ slightly in thickness, so some are loose.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It works on phones held upright and on PC. On a phone drag blocks with your finger, and on PC with the mouse.

How do I play?

Press and drag a block to push or pull it out. When it comes out, tap a glowing spot on top to stack it. Drag an empty spot to turn around the tower.

Who do I play against alone?

You take turns with a cat. The cat finds loose blocks better as the stages go up, and clearing STAGE 30 shows the ending.

Can two people play?

Yes. Pick 2 PLAYERS to take turns on one device.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to pick up where you left off. Nothing is sent to a server.
