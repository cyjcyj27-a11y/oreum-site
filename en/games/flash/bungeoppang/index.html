BUNGEOPPANG 붕어빵

🎮 Play now

BUNGEOPPANG is an idle shop game where you bake Korean fish-shaped bread — bungeoppang — at a winter street stall and grow the cart into a shop.

It is snowing, and you are looking out from inside the tent. Tap an empty slot in the cast iron mould on the left and batter goes in; tap it again once it turns golden and it sells. You start with four moulds and can reach twenty-four, and a stronger flame bakes faster. Buy the auto pour and the auto tongs and bread keeps coming out without you touching it.

The basin on the right is fish cake. Tap it to put skewers in the broth and customers walk up and buy one each. They toss money into the till, but that money is not yours yet — notes and coins pile up inside the box and you have to tap it to collect them. When the basin is empty, customers walk past.

Fillings start at red bean and go through custard, chocolate, sweet potato, cheese, pizza, mint choco and the golden fish. Unlock one in the dex with coins and you bake that filling; pricier ones sell for more. As total sales add up the street tent becomes a food truck and then a shop. A stray cat comes for the finished bread and snow piles on the counter — tap either one and it is handled.

Frequently asked questions

What are the controls?

Tap an empty mould and batter goes in; tap a baked fish bread and it sells. Tap the basin on the right to put fish cake skewers in the broth, and tap the till to turn the money piled inside into coins. Finger on a phone, mouse on a PC.

Does it earn while I am away?

Yes. Come back and every mould is full and the fish cakes have sold, with the money piled in the till. You still have to tap it to collect it. Snow piles up on the counter while you are gone, so clear that too.

How many fillings are there?

Eight: red bean, custard, chocolate, sweet potato, cheese, pizza, mint choco and the golden fish. Unlock one in the dex with coins and you bake that filling; pricier fillings sell for more. Now and then a golden one comes out of the mould and sells for twenty times the price.

What is the cat doing?

A stray cat steals a finished fish bread. Tap it while it is meowing and it leaves empty-pawed.

Is there an ending?

Total sales move you up from a street tent to a food truck and then a shop. The shop is the last tier and you keep growing it after that.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser and you carry on. Nothing is sent to a server.
