THE FART

🎮 Play now

THE FART is a free sneaky fart game where you let one rip only while nobody is looking, right in the browser on phone or PC.

Your fart meter keeps rising. Hold the screen or the fart button to let farts out and empty it. A ? above someone means they are about to turn around. If they see you farting, you are caught; if you hold it in until the meter is full, it bursts out. Either way it is GAME OVER.

Last until the time runs out to CLEAR the stage. Every three stages the place changes, from a classroom to a library, an elevator, a bus and a wedding, with more people turning around. If you lose you retry that stage, and NEW GAME starts over from stage 1.

Farting while someone is turning scores double, and stopping right before they look gives PERFECT. Emptying a big chunk at once gives NICE and GREAT.

Frequently asked questions

What are the controls?

Hold to fart and let go to stop. On a phone, turn it sideways and hold the screen or the fart button at the bottom right. On a PC, hold the space bar or the mouse button.

When do I get caught?

A ? above someone's head means they are starting to turn around. If their face is looking your way while you are farting, you are caught. While the ? is showing you are still safe, so let go then. Hold it in too long and the meter fills up and it bursts out.

Is it hard? How many stages are there?

Stages 1 to 3 fill slowly and people turn around slowly, so you can clear them on your first try. The stages never end. The place changes every three stages with more people turning around, and after the wedding it starts again from the classroom, faster.

Can I turn the fart sounds off?

Yes. The speaker button at the top right turns sound off and on. The setting is saved in the browser, so it stays quiet next time.

Is it free? Do I need to install anything?

It is free. No payments, and pressing Play now starts it in the browser. There is nothing to install and no sign-up; your stage and best score are saved in that browser.
