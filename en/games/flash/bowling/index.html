3D Bowling Game

🎮 Play now

BOWLING is a free 3D bowling game where you flick the ball with your finger to knock down pins. It runs in your browser with no download, on PC and mobile.

Drag the ball sideways to pick your spot, then flick up to send it down the lane. A faster flick rolls harder, and curving the end of your flick makes the ball hook. Angle it in between the 1 and 3 pins for your best shot at a strike. Pins fly off with the force and direction of each hit and knock each other down.

Each stage is one 10-frame game with a target score. Beat it to move on. Stage 1 asks for 50 points and stage 60 asks for 200. Every 12 stages the alley changes colors, from a city night to starry space, the sea, snow and a galaxy. Beat stage 60 to see the CHAMPION screen, and keep playing after that.

Collect coins to buy 11 more bowling balls for 12 in all, each with its own weight, power and hook. There are 12 missions such as a double, a turkey, a split pickup and a perfect 300. Pick 2 PLAYERS to take turns with a friend on one device.

Frequently asked questions

Is this bowling game free? Do I need to download anything?

It is free. You can play to the end without paying, and pressing Play now starts it in the browser. There is nothing to download and no sign-up.

How do I roll the ball?

Drag the ball sideways to pick where you stand, then flick up to roll it. A faster flick rolls harder, and curving the end of your flick makes the ball hook that way. On PC, do the same with the mouse, or use the left and right arrow keys and press Space.

How many stages are there?

Each stage is one 10-frame game, and there are 60 stages. Stage 1 asks for 50 points and stage 60 asks for 200. Beat stage 60 to see the CHAMPION screen, and the game keeps going after that with slowly rising targets.

Does it use real bowling scoring?

Yes. A strike adds your next two balls, a spare adds your next ball, and a strike or spare in the 10th frame earns an extra roll. A perfect game is 300.

Can two people play?

Yes. Pick 2 PLAYERS and take turns frame by frame on one phone or PC. Both scores show on the score sheet.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It is made for holding your phone upright, and it also works sideways. On PC, use the mouse or keyboard.

Is my progress saved?

Your stage, coins, balls, missions and the game in progress are saved in your browser. Open it again on the same device and browser to pick up from the frame you were on.
