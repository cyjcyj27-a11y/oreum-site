KINGWANGZZANG

🎮 Play now

KINGWANGZZANG is a free burger cooking game where you grill patties and stack burgers to each customer's order, right in the browser on phone or PC.

Drop raw patties on the griddle and they start to sizzle. Watch the doneness bar, flip once, and move each patty to the board when it reaches the rare, medium or well-done the customer asked for. Chicken and shrimp patties and fries come out of the fryer.

Every customer orders differently: "No pickles, please", "Mustard instead", "Lots of ketchup". The order row on the wall lays out every ingredient from the bottom bun to the top bun and ticks each one you add, and the closer you follow it, the more stars and coins you earn.

You serve up to two customers at once on two boards. More customers come each day, and 12 burgers unlock one by one, from the hamburger, cheeseburger and bulgogi burger up to the shrimp burger, avocado burger and a three-patty tower burger. Spend coins on grill space, heat, a fryer, seats and a sign, and grow a food truck into a burger legend. Earn five stars on all 12 burgers as well, and you reach the ending.

Frequently asked questions

What are the controls?

Everything is a tap or a click. Tap the raw patty tray to put a patty on the griddle, tap the patty once to flip it and again to move it to the board. Tap ingredient trays and sauce bottles to stack them on the burger, and tap the bun basket for the bottom and top buns.

How do I get rare, medium or well-done?

Each patty has a doneness bar. Pink is rare, orange is medium and brown is well-done; flip or move the patty when the arrow reaches the color the customer asked for. A burnt patty costs a lot of stars.

How do two customers work?

The two order rows on the wall are numbered to match the two boards in front. Tap a row or a board to stack on it, and covering the burger with the top bun sends it to the customer with that number.

Is it hard?

Day one has three customers and only the hamburger, and the next thing to tap glows softly. A patty that is slightly off still earns five stars, and menus and requests grow slowly over the days.

Does it end?

Yes. Earn five stars on all 12 burgers and grow your shop into a burger legend, and the ending plays. After the ending, New Game starts over from day one.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. On a phone, turn it sideways and tap; on PC, click with the mouse. All art and sound are made in code, so the whole game is under 1 MB and opens fast.

Is it free? Do I need to install anything?

It is free. You can play to the end without paying, and pressing Play now starts it in the browser. There is nothing to install and no sign-up; your coins, menus and shop level are saved in that browser.
