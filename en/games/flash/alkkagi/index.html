ALKKAGI

🎮 Play now

ALKKAGI is a game where you flick Go stones to knock the other side's stones off the board.

Five or six stones of each colour face each other on a Go board. Press on one of your stones, pull back and release; it shoots the opposite way, and the farther you pull the harder it goes. Players alternate one flick each, and a stone that leaves the board is gone. Knock off all of the opponent's stones to win.

Each stage the opponent flicks more accurately and the stone formation changes. On even stages the opponent goes first. There is no last stage; the stage you reach is your record.

Press 2 PLAYERS on the title screen and two people take turns on one phone or PC. Knock off two stones in one flick for a DOUBLE, three for a TRIPLE.

Frequently asked questions

What are the controls?

Press on one of your stones, pull back and release; the stone shoots the opposite way, and the farther you pull the harder it goes. Players alternate one flick each. A stone that leaves the board is gone. Knock off all of the opponent's stones to win; lose all of yours and it is game over.

How many stages are there?

There is no last stage. Each stage the opponent flicks more accurately and the stone formation changes. On even stages the opponent goes first. The stage you reach is your record.

Can two people play?

Yes. Press 2 PLAYERS on the title screen and two people take turns on one phone or PC. Whichever colour still has stones wins.

How does it work on a phone?

Hold the phone upright and pull a stone back with your finger, then let go. On PC you do the same with the mouse.
