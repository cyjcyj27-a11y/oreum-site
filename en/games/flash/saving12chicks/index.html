Saving 12 Chicks

🎮 Play now

Saving 12 Chicks is a free browser game where a rooster rescues 12 chicks on the way home. An old-school arcade game made by Oreum Games — no install, play instantly in your browser.

The rooster crosses six zones over 60 stages: farmyard, fields, country road, stream, village and a night road. Every 5th stage hides a chick peeking out of a pile of straw beside the road — peck it open and the chick pops out and follows behind you. Reach the signpost at the end of a stage to clear it, and the next stage starts on its own without pressing anything.

Dogs, cats, crows, snakes, foxes and cars appear one by one as the stages go on. Jump on most of them from above to bounce off and send them running, or roll a log into them, or push a crate into water to make a stepping stone. Getting hit costs a heart, but walking over grain or pecking open a worm fills a feeding meter, and every 3 bits of food refills one heart.

Jump over fences and rocks, and hold Space after jumping to glide down slowly. When the daylight runs out, night falls and a fox starts chasing you. Rescue all 12 chicks and reach home on stage 60 to watch the ending in the farmyard at dawn, with the hen and every chick waiting.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install Saving 12 Chicks?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts right away. No app install, no sign-up.

Is Saving 12 Chicks free?

Yes, it's completely free. You can play all the way through with no payments.

How do you play it?

The rooster walks along 60 stages of farm road. Reach the signpost at the right edge to clear a stage. Every 5th stage hides a chick inside a pile of straw — peck it open and the chick follows you. Rescue all 12 chicks and the door home opens on stage 60.

What are the controls?

Arrow keys to move, Shift to run. Space jumps (hold it to glide), X pecks. On phone, use the left pad to move and the right-side buttons to jump and peck.

How do I heal?

Walk over grain or peck open a worm to fill a feeding meter. Every 3 bits of food refills one heart. The meter stops filling once your hearts are full.

What obstacles are there?

Dogs, cats, crows, snakes, foxes and cars appear one by one as the stages go on. Jump on most of them from above to bounce off and send them running.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. Play in landscape on your phone — a pad on the left to move, and buttons on the right to jump and peck.

Is my progress saved?

Progress is saved in your browser. Reopen it on the same device and browser to continue. Nothing is sent to a server.
