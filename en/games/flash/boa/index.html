BOA

🎮 Play now

BOA is a snake game where you travel the Little Prince's seven stars, eat to grow, and swallow an elephant. Free, in the browser, no install.

You start in the jungle. The snake glides after your finger or mouse and grows longer with every frog it eats. Eat the set number and an elephant appears; swallow it and a bump travels down your body to the tail. Then Drawing No. 1, the one that looks like a hat, turns into Drawing No. 2 with the elephant showing inside, and that star is done. Touch a wall, your own body or an obstacle and it is GAME OVER; RETRY starts you on that star again.

Each star has its own rule. On B-612 you circle a round planet eating baobab sprouts before they become trees; four trees and the star breaks. On the king's star you may only eat what his speech bubble orders, and a wrong bite shrinks you. On the tippler's star a bottle reverses your controls for four seconds and tilts the screen. On the businessman's star you collect 30 falling stars in 40 seconds, and on the lamplighter's star day and night switch faster and faster, with only your head and the lamps visible at night. On Earth, the last star, a yellow snake chases you while you eat water drops and swallow the elephant.

There is one control. On PC the snake follows the mouse, and the arrow keys work too; space or click is DASH. On a phone the pad on the left steers and the button on the right is DASH. Clear all seven stars and the game ends. Your progress and best score are saved in the browser.

Frequently asked questions

What are the controls?

The snake keeps moving toward your mouse or finger, and the arrow keys work too. Space, click or the right-hand button is DASH. Touch a wall, your own body or an obstacle and it is over.

Is my progress saved?

In your browser. The star you reached and your best score stay, and the STAGE button on the title screen continues from there. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I swallow the elephant?

Eat the set number of food items on each star and an elephant appears; touch it with your head to swallow it whole. B-612 has three elephants.

What changes from star to star?

The jungle is the basic rule. B-612 is pulling baobab sprouts, the king's star is eating only what he orders, the tippler's star reverses your controls, the businessman's star has a 40-second limit, the lamplighter's star switches day and night, and Earth has a yellow snake chasing you. Clear Earth and the game ends.
