Double Parking Escape

🎮 Play now

Double Parking Escape is a free browser game where you slide blocking cars out of the way and drive your own car out of a double parked garage. No download, it starts right in your browser.

A double parked apartment garage fills a 6 by 6 board. Cars only move forward and back, so you drag the cars in your way to open a path. When the road from your red car to the exit is clear, the barrier lifts and your car drives out. You can drag a car, or tap it once to send it as far forward as it can go.

Every level is one workday. There are 100 levels from January 1 to April 10, and the clock starts at 8:58:30 the moment a level begins, so you have 1 minute 30 seconds before it hits 9:00:00. Get out before that and you are on time. Miss it and the day counts as late. Finish close to the target move count to earn up to three stars.

The first level has three cars and takes a single move. The minimum move count rises by at most one move per level until the last level on basement floor 9 takes 32 moves, and the cars grow from 3 to 14. Finish all 100 and the number of late days decides whether you are promoted to Team Leader, Manager or Assistant Manager, or stay on staff.

Frequently asked questions

Does the level end after 1 minute 30 seconds?

No. Once the clock passes 9:00:00 it turns red and you can keep going. The day is marked late, and if you replay the level and get out in time it becomes on time.

How is the ending decided?

After all 100 levels the game counts your late days. 10 or fewer makes you Team Leader, 25 or fewer Manager, 50 or fewer Assistant Manager, and more than that keeps you on staff.

How do I get stars?

Finish within two moves of the target move count for three stars. More moves than that drops you to two stars or one. Stars only count moves, not time.

Why can a car not move sideways?

Just like a real garage, cars only move forward and back. Cars facing left or right slide sideways on the board, and cars facing up or down slide up and down. Push a car into a blocked spot and it bumps and stays where it is.

Can I undo a wrong move?

UNDO takes back your last move, one at a time. An undo also counts as a move. To start over press RETRY, which also resets the clock to 8:58:30.

How do levels unlock?

Clearing a level opens the next one. There are 10 levels on each floor from 1F down to B9, and you can replay any cleared level from STAGE.
