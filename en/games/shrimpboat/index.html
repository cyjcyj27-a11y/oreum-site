Shrimp Boat

🎮 Play now

Shrimp Boat is a free 3D game where the spoiled youngest son of a chaebol family, shipped off on a shrimp boat, builds a raft and escapes to an island within 30 days. No download, it starts right in your browser.

We thought it was the story of a spoiled youngest son of a chaebol family who boards a shrimp boat and turns his life around. Turns out, even here, the brat is scheming.

After three years in America partying on his father’s card instead of studying, the youngest son is carried to the harbor dead drunk. His father hands him to his friend the captain and tells him to keep the boy off land for a whole year. That night the boat leaves for the open sea.

The day starts at 4 AM. Hold the net winch lever to haul the net while the tension needle swings with the waves. Keep hauling in the red and shrimp fall out of the net. Hold on longer and the net tears, the whole catch spills, and the captain runs over and slaps you.

Dump the net on the sorting table, then fling shrimp into the blue basket and toss the junk overboard: fish, crabs, starfish, cans and even a slipper. Pour the basket into the fish hold, scrub the grime off the deck with a long brush and splash it with seawater. From day 2 you also cook the captain his ramen. Miss the 8 PM deadline and the captain beats you, and that night is gone.

Finish the work and it jumps to 9 PM. While the captain snores in the wheelhouse, carry raft parts one at a time to the tarp at the bow: pallets, floats, rope, an oar, a life vest, a water jug and a compass, 12 in all. Running or carrying heavy things makes noise, and if the captain wakes up he patrols the deck with a flashlight. Get caught in the beam and he takes what you are holding and the night is over.

Every 5 days the boat anchors near an island. If all 12 parts are under the tarp that night, launch the raft and row to the beach, alternating left and right strokes. Stay in the boat’s searchlight too long and the boat gives chase. Fail to get off within 30 days and you get the one-more-year ending.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if the net tears?

If you keep hauling with the tension needle in the red for more than about a second, the net tears. The whole catch spills into the sea, the captain runs over and slaps you twice, and you have to haul the net again from the start.

What happens if I do not finish the work by 8 PM?

The captain beats you and that night is skipped, so the next morning starts right away. You lose one night of collecting raft parts.

What happens if the captain catches me?

If his flashlight catches you while holding a part or standing by the raft, he takes the part, hits you and the night ends. Empty-handed, he just tells you to go to bed. Parts already under the tarp stay there.

When can I escape?

On the nights of days 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 the boat anchors near an island. If all 12 parts are under the tarp that night, you can launch the raft.

What if I do not escape in 30 days?

It ends with you stuck on the boat for one more year. The save is cleared at the end and you start over from day 1.
