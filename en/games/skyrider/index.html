SKY RIDER

🎮 Play now

Sky Rider is a free delivery game: fly a motorbike through a village of stone pillars above the clouds and deliver food. It runs in your browser with nothing to install.

When an order comes in at the restaurant, check the map and find the customer's house. Land slowly on the green pad in front of the house to complete the delivery; miss the time limit and you only get half the fee. There are ten customers — a painter, a novelist, a film director, a singer, an actor, a YouTuber, a CEO, a footballer, a webtoon artist and a photographer — and every house looks different and has its own lines.

Fuel burns as you ride, and you fill up at the four gas stations to the north, east, south and west. Below 30% a warning sounds, and when it runs out you fall. Hitting flocks of crows or cliffs wears down your durability, and when it is gone the bike flips over. Even if you crash, the coins you earned stay.

There are five bikes: scooter, off-road, supersport, electric bike and hoverbike. You buy them at the motor shop next to the north gas station. Each step up is faster, tougher and pays more per delivery, but brings more crows too. The hoverbike is solar-powered, so it needs no fuel and does not fall when it stops.

FAQ

Do I need to install Sky Rider?

No. Open the link in your browser and it starts. No app, no sign-up.

Is Sky Rider free?

Yes, completely free. No payments and nothing to install.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. On a phone you drag the screen to steer and use the UP and DOWN buttons to climb and descend. On a PC you use the arrow keys.

How do I play?

Arrow keys to ride and turn. Space to climb, Shift to descend. T opens the map. Stop in front of the motor shop and a SHOP button appears. Drag with the mouse to turn the camera.

Is my progress saved?

Your coins and the bikes you bought are saved in your browser automatically. Open it again on the same device and browser to continue. Nothing is sent to a server.
