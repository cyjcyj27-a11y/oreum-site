Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 9, 2026 · Last updated: August 14, 2026

Oreum Games ("the Company") values your privacy and operates its games and website on the principle of not collecting personal information. This policy applies to all games provided by Oreum Games and to the oreumgames.com website.

Summary
  • No sign-up, no login.
  • We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Game progress is stored only on your device.
  • Only what you post on the Community board is kept by the Company.

1. Personal Information We Collect

The Company does not directly collect any personal information, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, or payment details. No sign-up or login is required to use our games or website.

2. Storage of Game Data

Game progress (in-game currency, items, progression, settings, and so on) is stored only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is never transmitted externally, including to Company servers. Deleting your browser data also deletes your game progress, and you can do this yourself at any time.

3. Community Board

When you leave a post on the Community board, the following is stored with our storage provider (Supabase) and shown publicly to anyone who visits.

  • The photo you upload
  • The text you write (up to 200 characters)
  • The name you enter (optional)
  • The 4 digits used to delete your post, stored in a scrambled form that even the Company cannot read
  • The time you posted

Because there are no accounts, the Company cannot tell who posted what. Since posts are public, please do not upload photos showing faces, or details such as your name or phone number.

You can delete your own post at any time using your 4 digits. If you forget them, or if a post is spam, abusive, or infringes someone's rights, contact us at the address below and we will remove it. Deleting a post also deletes its photo.

Posts are held on servers operated by Supabase Inc. until they are deleted or the board is closed.

4. Advertising

If a game includes advertising, the advertising provider (e.g., Google AdMob) may collect advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and similar data to serve ads and measure performance. This is governed by the advertising provider's own privacy policy; the Company does not receive or store that information.

  • Google advertising policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Website Visit Statistics

To understand how many people visit the website, the Company uses two statistics tools. Neither collects or stores information that could identify an individual, such as names or email addresses.

  • Abacus counts the visitor numbers shown on the site. It does not use cookies.
  • Google Analytics measures visitor countries and referral sources. It uses cookies, and the pages you visit, your country, referral source, and device type are sent to Google.

If you are visiting from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, no cookies are stored and no advertising data is collected until you have given consent.

If you prefer not to be measured by Google Analytics, you can install the opt-out add-on provided by Google.

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Opt-out add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

6. Children Under 14

The Company's games are available to all ages, including children under 14. Because we collect no personal information at all, no child's personal information is ever stored. If advertising is included, we apply the advertising provider's child-directed settings so that ads unsuitable for children are not shown.

7. Third-Party Disclosure and Outsourcing

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties. For the Community board, storage of the photos and text you post is handled on our behalf by Supabase Inc.

8. Your Rights

You can view and delete the game data stored on your device at any time through your browser settings. For any other privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at the address below and we will respond without delay.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated content and its effective date will be posted on this page.
