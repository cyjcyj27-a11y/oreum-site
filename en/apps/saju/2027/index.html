What kind of year is 2027, the Year of the Fire Goat?

2027 is Ding-Wei (丁未), the Year of the Fire Goat. The heavenly stem Ding is yin fire, the fire of a candle or lamp that lights what is close by. The earthly branch Wei is the warm, dry earth at the end of summer, and it holds Ding Fire, Eul Wood and Gi Earth inside: earth on the surface, with an ember and tree roots underneath.

Fire gives birth to earth, so after the blazing Fire Horse year of 2026, 2027 is read as the year things settle and take shape. It is closer to a year of harvesting and firming up than of starting over. The effect is not the same for everyone, though. For some day masters fire and earth are support, for others they are pressure, so your 2027 depends on the stem of your birth day.

In saju and BaZi, 2027 begins at Lichun, February 4, 2027 at 10:46 Korea time. Before that moment it is still the Horse year. Lunar New Year, the usual start of the zodiac year, is February 6 in China and February 7 in Korea.

Samjae 2027: which signs are in the Three Calamities cycle?

Samjae is a Korean tradition: every sign passes through three years of Three Calamities once every twelve years. In 2027 the Pig, Rabbit and Goat are in Samjae, all three in the outgoing year. These signs enter Samjae in Snake, Horse and Goat years, so 2025 was the incoming year, 2026 the pressing year and 2027 the year it leaves. The outgoing year is read as a time to clear up what happened in the two years before.

Frequently asked questions

What Chinese zodiac year is 2027?
2027 is the Year of the Fire Goat, Ding-Wei (丁未) in the sixty-year cycle. The heavenly stem Ding is yin fire, like a candle or lamp, and the earthly branch Wei is the warm, dry earth of late summer. Fire feeding earth makes it a year when what you started takes shape and comes into your hands.
When does the 2027 Year of the Goat start?
Lunar New Year falls on February 6, 2027 in China and February 7 in Korea. Saju and BaZi change the year at Lichun, the start of spring, which is February 4, 2027 at 10:46 Korea time. People born between these dates may belong to different signs depending on which rule is used. This page uses Lichun for the overview and months, and the lunar calendar for Tojeong Bigyeol.
Which signs are in Samjae in 2027?
The Pig, Rabbit and Goat. Samjae is the Korean three-year Three Calamities cycle. Pig, Rabbit and Goat enter it in Snake, Horse and Goat years, so 2025 was the incoming year, 2026 the pressing year and 2027 is the outgoing year, the last of the three. The next Samjae begins in 2028 for the Tiger, Horse and Dog.
Why is my 2027 reading different from others born under the same sign?
Your zodiac sign comes from one character, the year of birth. This reading starts from your day master, the heavenly stem of your birth day, and looks at what the year's Ding Fire and Wei Earth become for it. Two Goats can have a Gyeong Metal day master, for whom 2027 is a year of rising in name, or a Gye Water day master, for whom chances and shake-ups arrive together.
What is saju and how is it related to BaZi?
Saju means four pillars in Korean. Like Chinese BaZi, it reads the year, month, day and hour of birth as four pairs of characters and interprets how their five elements interact. Korea adds its own traditions such as Samjae and the Tojeong Bigyeol New Year reading, and both are included here.

Do I need my birth time?
No. The overview and month by month forecast use the day master and day branch, so the birth date is enough. Adding the hour completes all four pillars and makes the month pillar exact for people born on the day a solar term changes.
