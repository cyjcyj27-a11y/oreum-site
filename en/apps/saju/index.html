What is MBTI Saju?

Saju × MBTI personality reading · Free

MBTI Saju reads the saju pillars built from your birth date next to MBTI, today's language for personality, on one screen. It pulls the eight characters from the traditional almanac, reads the day master, the five elements, the ten gods and the special stars, and sets that beside your MBTI so you can see where "you according to saju" and "you according to MBTI" overlap and where they part.

How the reading works

  • Almanac calculation — your birth date (and hour) becomes eight characters: the year, month, day and hour pillars.
  • Day master — the centre of the eight. The character that stands for you, and the basis of your temperament.
  • Balance of the five elements — where wood, fire, earth, metal and water lean tells you which way your energy runs.
  • The ten gods — wealth, authority, resource, output and peer stars draw the pattern of your relationships and appetites.
  • Special stars — the particular currents laid over a chart, such as the traveller star, the peach blossom star and the artist star.
  • The MBTI link — all of the above is rewritten in the language of the four MBTI axes (E/I, S/N, T/F, J/P), so the personality your saju describes can be compared with the MBTI you know yourself by.

An example reading

Someone whose day master is Bright Fire (丙火) is read like the sun at midday — a temperament that shows itself openly. If their elements lean towards fire and wood, they start quickly and speak before they hold back; if the output stars are strong among the ten gods, the urge to make things and show them is large. And if the MBTI they know themselves by is INFP — the gap between a saju that reaches outward and an MBTI that pools inward becomes the story itself. MBTI Saju puts that gap into the reading too.

Frequently asked

Is it free?
Yes. A free saju reading you open in your browser — no sign-up, no install.
What if I do not know my birth time?
We read the remaining six characters without the hour pillar. Knowing the time only makes it more detailed.
Do I need to take an MBTI test first?
No. Pick your MBTI if you know it; if you do not, the saju reading alone still gives you a result.
Is my birth date stored?
Nothing is sent to a server. The calculation happens inside your browser.
Is this just for fun?
It follows the frame of the classical texts, but it is built to be a mirror for understanding your own leanings. The decisions that matter are always yours.
