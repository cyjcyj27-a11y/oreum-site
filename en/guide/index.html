Play Guide

Lulu the Cat's Jeju Life · A guide for first-time players

Play Now

Lulu's Goal

Lulu spent everything she had on an empty house in a Jeju village
The goal: build a fortune of ₩100 million
Fix up the old house and buy every field you've been renting.
Check your progress in the 🎒 bag at the bottom left.

Controls

Arrow keysWalk
FAll interactions (pick, talk, buy)
Shift · SpaceRun · Jump
A·D / W·S / Z·XLook around / up-down / zoom
MMap

On mobile, drag the left side of the screen to move and the right side to look around.
Action buttons are at the bottom right; underwater, drag the left side up or down to rise and dive.

Saving

Save with the 💾 button at the top right.
There are no accounts and no server, so your save lives only in the browser on this device.

  • Always open the game on the same device, in the same browser.
    Opening it in a different browser starts a new game.
  • Clearing browser data, cleaner apps, and incognito mode erase your save.
    Please avoid them.
  • Closing the tab, refreshing, and rebooting are safe.

In-Game Time

  • One day is 10 minutes.
    Time only passes while the window is open.
  • Even days are premium-citrus harvest days: Cheonhyehyang → Hallabong → Hwanggeumhyang.
  • Odd days bring a 30% typhoon chance.
    No diving during a typhoon.

Four Ways to Earn Money

🍊 Tangerine Picking: The Safest

Press F at a tree to fill your crate (20 fruits), then sell it at the shipping depot.
A container (₩200,000) lets you sell crates in batches of five, and they fetch a better price.

🤿 Haenyeo Diving: Good Money, Real Danger

Buy a net bag (₩10,000), put it on, and enter the water at the end of the harbor breakwater.
You must fill all 10 slots and come ashore to sell.
Night diving pays double.

Run out of breath, and the rescue helicopter carries you away.
The hospital bill is ₩300,000, and you lose your net bag with everything in it.
Always watch your breath gauge.

🐴 Pony Racing: Pure Luck

Feed your pony 3 carrots and it will enter a race.
Tired of farming? Here's your shot at one big win.

🌱 Field Farming: Plant It and It Grows

Rent one of the four plots around your yard from the mayor (₩500,000 a year) or buy it outright (₩10,000,000).
Sow seeds and just wait, no watering needed.
Half of a rented field's harvest goes to the mayor.

🏚 Repairing and Decorating Your House

Fix the house with a roof, gate, and paint from the store, then furnish it with 25 kinds of furniture, flooring, and wallpaper.
Fix the house up nicely, and your story with Munam, the neighbor who wears a suit every day, begins.

Good to Know

  • Start with tangerines.
    It's the only work you can do with no money down.
  • Don't leave your fields empty.
    Sowing before you head out always pays.
  • As soon as you've saved ₩10,000 for a net bag, start diving.
    Your income jumps.
