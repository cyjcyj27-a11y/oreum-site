When Reporting a Bug

Including the details below helps us fix things much faster.

  • Which game (e.g., Lulu the Cat's Jeju Life)
  • What you were doing when it happened (e.g., surfacing after a dive)
  • Your device and browser (e.g., Galaxy S23, Chrome)
  • If possible, a screenshot or a short video

Frequently Asked Questions

My saved game disappeared

Game saves are stored only in your device's browser, not on a server. If you clear your browsing data or cookies, play in private mode, or open the game on a different device or browser, your previous save can't be loaded.

The game won't open / The screen is black

As a 3D game, it depends on your device's performance and browser settings. Try opening it in the latest Chrome or Safari and refresh the page. If it still won't work, email us at the address above along with your device name.

There's no sound

On mobile, sound starts after you tap the screen once. Please also check your silent mode (ring switch).

Does it cost money?

Oreum Games titles are free. There is nothing to pay for.
